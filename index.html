Tips

  • Works with YouTube (videos, live, shorts, playlists), Twitch, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Kick, and direct .mp4 / .m3u8 links.
  • Other web pages open full-window too, but some sites refuse to load inside another page.
  • While playing, the faint buttons in the top-left corner go back or zoom. Zoom crops black bars.
  • Press Circle (browser back) to return here.
